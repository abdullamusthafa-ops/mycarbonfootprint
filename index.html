What is a Carbon Footprint?

Your carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases you generate through everyday activities like using electricity, driving, flying, and even what you eat. These emissions contribute to climate change, but by being aware of your footprint, you can make conscious decisions that reduce your impact on the planet.
